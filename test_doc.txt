On the morning of Tuesday, October 13, 2026, at approximately 07:42 ET, the Security Operations Center received an automated alert (ticket INC-555-12-0093) indicating anomalous outbound traffic from workstation HFL-WS-0447, assigned to accounts-payable specialist Marisol Okonkwo-Reyes (employee ID E-204718, DOB 03/14/1988), whose corporate address is m.okonkwo.reyes@example.com and whose listed mobile number is (412) 555-0147; the workstation, MAC address 00:1A:2B:3C:4D:5E, was observed sending roughly 2.3 GB over an encrypted channel to external host 203.0.113.42 and, after a brief failover, to 198.51.100.7 and the IPv6 endpoint 2001:db8::ff00:42:8329, none of which appear on the approved vendor list, although it should be noted that the string 10.4.2.118 appearing in the alert header refers to the firmware build of the perimeter appliance and not to any network address. Ms. Okonkwo-Reyes, interviewed later that day in Conference Room "Washington" on the third floor, stated that she had received a phone call the previous Friday from a man identifying himself as "Bill Hunter from the bank," who asked her to "verify" the company card on file and read back the number 4111 1111 1111 1111 with expiration 09/29 and CVV 737, which she declined to do, but she admitted that she later forwarded a spreadsheet titled Q3_vendor_remit_FINAL(2).xlsx to an address she believed belonged to her supervisor, Theodore "Teddy" Varga, written in the email as tvarga at example dot org, when in fact the actual recipient was t.varga@examp1e.org (note the numeral one substituted for the letter "l"); Mr. Varga's legitimate address is t.varga@example.org and he can be reached at extension 4471 or on his personal cell, which he gave verbally during interview as "four-one-two, five-five-five, zero-one-six-two." Lindqvist, residing at 1427 Fernhollow Lane, Apt 3B, Pittsburgh, PA 15217, SSN 900-31-8826, PA driver's license 99 412 087, born on the fourth of July, 1979, together with her direct-deposit details (routing 011000015, account 000123456789) and a second card on file ending in 4444, which forensics later matched to the test-issuer number 5555-5555-5555-4444 used in a misconfigured staging environment that had, for reasons still under review, been copied into production; separately, the same tab listed an international supplier contact, Dr. Priya Raman of Raman Precision Components, with IBAN GB82 WEST 1234 5698 7654 32 and a contact email encoded in a notes cell as cHJpeWEucmFtYW5AZXhhbXBsZS5uZXQ= (base64), which decodes to a standard address at example.net, as well as her assistant, a gentleman simply named Paris, who should not be confused with the city of Paris, France, where the supplier's parent company is headquartered at 14 Rue de l'Exemple, 75008 — a point the reviewing analyst flagged because an earlier automated scan had misclassified both.
